01 / Concept illustration
Biomedical systems01

Pulmoaid

A respiratory pressure monitoring prototype with BLE transmission, real-time mobile visualization and CSV export.

Technical overview

ESP32-S3, MPRLS pressure sensor, I²C and BLE. Designed for exploratory monitoring and training; not a validated medical device.

02 / Concept illustration
Robotics / Control02

Pan–tilt tracking

Two-axis tracking prototype using serial commands, servo control and exponential smoothing to reduce jitter.

Technical overview

ESP32, PCA9685 servo driver, two SG90 servos and a USB serial interface. Exponential smoothing is applied to target angles.

03 / Concept illustration
Instrumentation / Feedback03

Magnetic field system

Closed-loop magnetic field control using a coil system, PWM and magnetometer feedback.

Technical overview

ESP32-based control and RM3100 magnetometer measurements, with experimental comparison to theoretical field calculations. Public media and technical details are subject to permission.

04 / Concept illustration
Applied ML / Research04

CivicPulse-STGNN

Empirical benchmarking of spatiotemporal graph learning for urban complaint prediction against simpler baselines.

Research findings

Persistence outperformed the tested neural models. The work emphasizes baseline strength, graph ablations and careful temporal evaluation rather than claiming a positive graph-learning result. Manuscript status: research report, not a peer-reviewed publication.
